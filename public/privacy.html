Privacy in Grammar Casino

Grammar Casino is a classroom grammar game. The money in it is pretend. This page says what the game keeps and where it goes.

Students

  • No account and no email. You join with a room code and a nickname.
  • Use a nickname or first name only. Do not type personal information in any answer.
  • Your phone keeps a random identifier in its own storage, so you can get your seat back after a reload. It is not linked to who you are.
  • While a room is open, your nickname, character, bets and answers are shared with the teacher's screen through the game's database.

Marking and speech

  • To mark typed answers and write new sentences, the game sends the sentence and the typed answers to an AI service (CoArena first, then NVIDIA, Groq or Google Gemini as backups). Nicknames are never sent.
  • To read a sentence aloud, only the sentence text is sent to the speech service.

Teachers

  • A teacher can sign in with Google to save games and reports in the cloud. Those reports hold nicknames and results, and only that teacher can open them.
  • The game's debug log stays on the teacher's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless the teacher downloads the file and shares it.

How long things are kept

  • A game saved on a computer stays there until the teacher starts a new class or clears the browser.
  • Room data is not yet deleted automatically. Deleting rooms 24 hours after the last activity is being added.

Questions about your data? Ask your teacher. This notice describes how the game works; it is not legal advice.
